MASTER CATALOG — FINAL UX + VARIANT + DATA FIXES
=================================================

We have already implemented the Master Catalog architecture and populated the initial Master Catalog.

DO NOT redesign the underlying Master Catalog architecture.

DO NOT create a replacement store_products table.

DO NOT modify authentication.

DO NOT modify checkout, orders, reorder, payments, Delivery Partner, or stock reservation/deduction.

DO NOT delete existing legacy store/vendor data.

DO NOT delete Master Categories or Master Products.

This task is ONLY to fix and complete the Master Catalog, Vendor Catalog, Customer Global Search, missing-product flow, and multi-variant selling configuration based on the actual browser verification.

The goal is to make the Master Catalog fully usable by a normal supermarket vendor without requiring technical knowledge or knowledge of the platform's category structure.


=================================================
1. MASTER CATALOG MUST CONTAIN REAL MASTER DATA
=================================================

The Master Catalog must contain actual platform-wide grocery Master Categories and Master Products.

Master Catalog is NOT the same thing as the vendor's existing "My Store Products".

Ensure the seeded Master Catalog data is actually stored in:

- master_categories
- master_products

and is returned by the Vendor Master Catalog API.

The Vendor Master Catalog must show actual Master Products immediately when opened.

Do not rely on legacy/store-owned categories or products as Master Catalog data.

At minimum, retain/populate the agreed supermarket MVP categories and representative grocery products.

The categories should include:

1. Rice & Grains
2. Atta & Flours
3. Dals & Pulses
4. Cooking Oils & Ghee
5. Spices & Masalas
6. Salt & Sugar
7. Dairy & Milk Products
8. Eggs
9. Fruits
10. Vegetables
11. Biscuits & Cookies
12. Bakery
13. Snacks
14. Chocolates & Sweets
15. Beverages
16. Tea & Coffee
17. Breakfast & Cereals
18. Packaged Foods
19. Noodles & Pasta
20. Sauces & Spreads
21. Pickles
22. Baby Food
23. Personal Care
24. Household Cleaning
25. Home & Kitchen Essentials

Populate meaningful common grocery Master Products under these categories.

Do not create every pack size as a separate Master Product.

Example:

MASTER PRODUCT:
Basmati Rice

NOT:
Basmati Rice 500g
Basmati Rice 1kg
Basmati Rice 2kg
Basmati Rice 5kg

Those must be selling variants of the same product.


=================================================
2. VENDOR GLOBAL SEARCH
=================================================

Vendor Master Catalog search must be GLOBAL.

The vendor must NOT need to know the category in which a product exists.

Category is an optional filter only.

The search should work across the complete eligible Master Catalog.

Examples:

Search:
rice

Should find relevant rice products.

Search:
milk

Should find relevant milk products.

Search:
eggs

Should find relevant egg products.

Search:
amul

Should find relevant products containing that brand.

The vendor should be able to search by relevant product name, brand, and product details.

Do not require category selection before search.

Do not limit search to the selected category.

Keep backend pagination.


=================================================
3. CUSTOMER GLOBAL SEARCH
=================================================

Customer global search should also be user-friendly.

Customers should NOT need to know the category before searching.

Customer can search globally for:

- product name
- brand
- relevant product details

Category can be an optional filter.

However, actual purchasing remains store-specific.

Only show/present purchasable store-specific results according to existing:

- eligible store rules
- saved-address pincode rules
- vendor/store availability
- variant availability
- server-side stock validation

Do not expose:

- exact stock quantity
- SKU
- external codes
- internal timestamps
- unnecessary internal identifiers

=================================================

The vendor must be able to create a missing product.

The vendor must NOT be forced to know the platform category.

When vendor clicks:

"Can't find your product?"

ADD NEW PRODUCT

Fields:

Product Name *
Brand
Product Type / Details
Description
Image

Category must NOT be mandatory at this stage.

Do NOT expose JSON fields.

Do NOT expose "Discovery Attributes" JSON input.

Do NOT ask the vendor to enter JSON.

Do NOT use technical terminology such as:

- platform identity
- canonical identity
- discovery JSON
- JSON attributes

Use normal supermarket terminology.


=================================================
5. DUPLICATE CHECK BEFORE NEW PRODUCT CREATION
=================================================

Before creating a new Master Product, perform a GLOBAL duplicate check.

The check must search the entire Master Catalog, not only one category.

Check both active and archived Master Products.

If exact/similar products are found, display them clearly.

Example:

"Possible matching products found"

- Basmati Rice
- Premium Basmati Rice
- Long Grain Basmati Rice

Vendor should be able to select an existing product instead.

If no suitable product exists, show:

"No matching product found."

Then require an explicit confirmation:

[ ] I checked the results and this product is not already in the catalog.

Only after the vendor checks this confirmation should:

"Confirm no match & create"

be enabled.

This duplicate confirmation is specifically intended to prevent accidental duplicate Master Products.

Keep the existing concurrency-safe creation/reuse logic.


=================================================
6. CATEGORY FOR VENDOR-CREATED PRODUCTS
=================================================

The vendor should NOT have to manually understand or select the platform taxonomy before duplicate checking.

If the platform cannot confidently determine the category for a newly created product:

DO NOT force the vendor to choose an arbitrary category.

Allow the product to enter the appropriate internal pending/unclassified state or admin classification flow.

The vendor should be able to continue to the store configuration without being blocked by taxonomy knowledge.

Do not create incorrect category mappings simply to satisfy a required field.

=================================================

There must be NO DELETE operation for:

- Master Products

15. PRICE VALIDATION
=================================================

A variant cannot be published without a valid selling price.

Price must be greater than zero for a customer-purchasable published variant.

Display a clear validation message when missing/invalid.

Do not allow the vendor to publish an incomplete commercial variant.


=================================================
16. STOCK BEHAVIOR
=================================================

Stock = 0 should NOT prevent publishing.

A vendor may publish a product that is temporarily out of stock.

Example:

Basmati Rice
1 kg
₹75
Stock: 0

The product remains configured but should be shown to customers as:

Out of stock / unavailable

and customer cannot purchase it.

Existing server-side stock validation remains authoritative.

DO NOT implement stock reservation/deduction in this task.


=================================================
17. AVAILABILITY
=================================================

Each variant should have:

Available / Unavailable

If unavailable, customers cannot purchase that variant.

The vendor should be able to change availability later.
