PHASE 2 — CUSTOMER SHOPPING EXPERIENCE
======================================

We have completed the Master Catalog and Vendor Product/Variant implementation.

The following are already implemented and must NOT be redesigned:

- Master Categories
- Master Products
- Vendor global Master Catalog search
- Vendor Add to My Store
- Missing-product creation
- Duplicate prevention
- Vendor product configuration
- Multiple selling variants
- Quantity + unit generated variant names
- g / kg / ml / L / piece / pack / dozen / bottle / box
- Vendor price
- Vendor stock
- Vendor availability
- Store-specific product listings
- Master Product archive behavior
- No Master Product deletion
- Customer catalog privacy rules

This phase is ONLY for the CUSTOMER SHOPPING EXPERIENCE.

DO NOT modify:
- authentication
- Master Catalog architecture
- vendor catalog creation
- vendor product management
- payments
- Delivery Partner
- stock reservation
- automatic stock deduction
- automatic unit conversion
- order lifecycle redesign
- reorder functionality unless required only for compatibility
- historical order data


=================================================
1. CUSTOMER GLOBAL SEARCH
=================================================

Customer must be able to search the grocery catalog globally.

Search must NOT require category selection.

Examples:

Search:
rice

should find relevant grocery products.

Search:
milk

should find relevant products.

Search:
eggs

should find relevant products.

Search:
amul

should find relevant products where applicable.

Search should work across the Master Catalog but customer results must still respect store availability and eligibility.

Category is an optional filter, not a prerequisite.

Provide:
- search input
- clear search
- optional category filter
- loading state
- no-results state
- error state
- pagination/infinite loading as appropriate

Do not expose internal Master Catalog implementation details.


=================================================
2. CUSTOMER STORE CONTEXT
=================================================

Customer purchasing remains STORE-SPECIFIC.

Global product discovery must NOT mean that a customer can purchase from an arbitrary vendor.

Results must respect the existing store eligibility rules, including the customer's saved-address pincode.

When a product is available from multiple eligible stores, present the appropriate store context.

Do not merge inventory or pricing between vendors.


=================================================
3. PRODUCT CARD
=================================================

Customer product cards should be clean and easy to understand.

Show only shopping-relevant information.

At minimum:

- Product image where available
- Product name
- Brand where available
- Store where applicable
- Starting price or selected variant price
- Availability state
- Add/select action

Do NOT show:

- SKU
- External code
- Exact internal stock quantity
- Internal timestamps
- Internal Master Product IDs
- Internal variant IDs


=================================================
4. MULTI-VARIANT PRODUCT CARD
=================================================

A product with multiple selling variants must NOT appear as multiple separate products.

Example:

Basmati Rice

Available variants:
- 500 g
- 1 kg
- 2 kg
- 5 kg

The customer should see ONE:

Basmati Rice

and then select the required selling option.

Do not create four product cards for the four variants.


=================================================
5. PRODUCT DETAILS SCREEN
=================================================

When customer opens a product, show:

Product name
Brand
Description where available
Image where available
Store context
Available selling variants

Example:

Basmati Rice

Choose an option:

○ 500 g — ₹40
○ 1 kg — ₹75
○ 2 kg — ₹145
○ 5 kg — ₹350

The customer must select a variant before adding it to cart when multiple variants exist.


=================================================
6. VARIANT DISPLAY
=================================================

Use the existing generated variant names.

Examples:

500 g
1 kg
2 kg
5 kg

Do not expose raw decimal formatting such as:

500.000

Use the shared quantity formatter already implemented.

Display prices consistently:

₹40
₹75
₹145
₹350

For decimal prices:

₹75.50

Do not expose unnecessary trailing zeroes.


=================================================
7. AVAILABILITY
=================================================

Customer-facing availability must be server-derived.

A variant is purchasable only when:

- variant is active
- variant is marked available
- current server-side stock permits purchase

If stock = 0:

Show:

Out of stock

Do NOT allow Add to Cart for that variant.

If vendor availability is OFF:

Show:

Currently unavailable

Do NOT allow Add to Cart.


=================================================
8. STOCK PRIVACY
=================================================

Customer must NOT see exact stock quantity.

Do not display:

Stock: 20

Instead display only customer-friendly availability such as:

Available
Out of stock
Currently unavailable

The backend remains authoritative for actual stock validation.

Do not implement stock reservation or stock deduction in this phase.


=================================================
9. ADD TO CART
=================================================

For a product with one variant:

Customer may use:

Add to Cart

provided that the variant is purchasable.

For a product with multiple variants:

Customer must select a variant first.

Then:

Add to Cart

The cart item must contain the correct:

- store
- product
- selected variant
- quantity
- current price

Preserve the existing cart architecture and one-store-cart rule.


=================================================
10. QUANTITY CONTROL
=================================================

After selecting a variant, customer should be able to select the desired purchase quantity.

Do not confuse:

PRODUCT VARIANT QUANTITY

with:

ORDER QUANTITY.

Example:

Variant:
1 kg

Customer order quantity:
3

means:

3 × 1 kg

It does NOT mean creating a new 3 kg variant.

Keep this distinction throughout the UI.


=================================================
11. CART PRESENTATION
=================================================

Cart must clearly display:

Product
Selected variant
Unit/size
Quantity ordered
Unit price
Line total

Example:

Basmati Rice
1 kg
Qty: 2
₹75 each
₹150

Do not display internal SKU/external codes.

Do not expose exact inventory.


=================================================
12. CART NAVIGATION
=================================================

Do NOT restore a permanent Cart bottom-navigation tab if the current design has removed it.

Keep the contextual cart behavior.

After adding an item:

Show a clear cart action such as:

View Cart

or:

Go to Cart

The customer must be able to continue shopping without being forced into the cart immediately.

Do not break existing navigation.


=================================================
13. ADDING MULTIPLE PRODUCTS
=================================================

Customer should be able to:

Search
→ Open product
→ Select variant
→ Add to Cart
→ Continue Shopping
→ Search another product
→ Add another product

while maintaining the existing one-store-cart rule.

If the customer attempts to add a product from another store, preserve the existing store-conflict handling.

Do not silently mix stores in one cart.


=================================================
14. PRICE CONSISTENCY
=================================================

Customer must see the current vendor/store variant price.

Do not use Master Product price because Master Products do not own commercial pricing.

Price belongs to:

Vendor Store Listing → Selling Variant

Historical order prices must remain untouched.
