PROJECT DIRECTION ALREADY APPROVED:
The marketplace must move from the current store-owned product/catalog architecture toward a platform-level MASTER CATALOG.

The intended business model is:

PLATFORM MASTER CATALOG
        ↓
Vendor selects existing master category/item
        ↓
"Add to My Store"
        ↓
Vendor-specific store product/selection
        ↓
Vendor manages price, stock, quantity, unit, variants, availability
        ↓
Customer sees only products selected by that particular store

IMPORTANT BUSINESS RULES:

1. Platform administrators/platform data should control the common grocery catalog.
2. Common grocery categories and products should NOT be independently created from scratch by every vendor.
3. Vendor A selecting a product must NOT automatically make that product available in Vendor B's store.
4. Vendor must explicitly select/add a master product to their own store.
5. Vendor-specific information must remain isolated per store.
6. Vendor must be able to manage their own:
   - selling price
   - stock
   - available quantity
   - selling unit
   - pack/quantity configuration
   - availability
   - variants where applicable
7. Customer catalog must show products belonging to the selected store only.
8. Existing historical order data, order item snapshots, IDs, prices, quantities, and historical records MUST remain intact during any future migration.
9. Existing architecture should be changed ADDITIVELY wherever possible rather than destructively replacing historical structures.
10. This Master Catalog work must remain separate from the Delivery Partner work and other future phases.

PREVIOUS AUDIT FINDINGS:

STEP 1 established that the current architecture has:
- Customer Expo application
- Vendor/admin dashboard
- Express/TypeScript backend
- Drizzle/PostgreSQL
- Supabase Auth/DB
- Existing store-owned categories/products
- Existing product_variants architecture
- Existing cart/order/reorder functionality
- No master_categories/master_products/store_products architecture currently identified
- Existing historical order/snapshot structures that must be protected

STEP 2 AUTHENTICATION ARCHITECTURE WAS APPROVED:

- Username is the login identifier.
- A private server-side username registry will resolve usernames to the authoritative Supabase identity.
- Vendor registration/approval remains a separate permission decision.
- Delivery Partner will be handled in a later dedicated phase.
- Do not modify authentication as part of this audit.
