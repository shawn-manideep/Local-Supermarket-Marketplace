=================================================
1. REORDER PURPOSE
=================================================

Customer should be able to easily purchase products from a previous order again.

Reorder does NOT mean immediately placing the previous order.

It means:

Previous Order
↓
Reorder / Buy Again
↓
Eligible previous items are added to the current cart
↓
Customer reviews current products/prices/availability
↓
Customer can modify cart
↓
Customer continues through the normal checkout flow


=================================================
2. NEVER PLACE ORDER IMMEDIATELY
=================================================

When customer clicks:

Reorder

DO NOT create a new order immediately.

DO NOT charge/payment immediately.

DO NOT bypass Cart.

Instead:

Add eligible items to the customer's current cart.

Then show:

"Items added to your cart"

with:

View Cart
Continue Shopping


=================================================
3. REORDER FROM ORDER HISTORY
=================================================

Customer should be able to access previous orders.

Where the current customer navigation already has:

Orders

do not break the existing order history.

Provide a clear Reorder / Buy Again action for eligible previous orders.

Example:

Order #1234
Completed
Basmati Rice
1 kg × 2
Milk
1 L × 2
Eggs
12 pieces × 1

[ Reorder ]

The customer can reorder the complete eligible order.


=================================================
4. REORDER INDIVIDUAL ITEMS
=================================================

Where practical, also support:

[ Buy Again ]

for individual previous products.

Example:

Basmati Rice
1 kg
₹75 at the time of previous order

[ Buy Again ]

The system should attempt to add that exact previous product/variant to the current cart.

Do not substitute another product automatically.


=================================================
5. EXACT PRODUCT + VARIANT IDENTITY
=================================================

Reorder must preserve the exact historical product/variant identity.

Example:

Previous order:

Basmati Rice
Variant: 1 kg

Reorder must attempt:

Basmati Rice
Variant: 1 kg

It must NOT silently change to:

500 g
2 kg
5 kg

It must NOT select another similar product.

It must NOT use only the product name to determine what to reorder.


=================================================
6. CURRENT PRICE
=================================================

Reorder must use the CURRENT vendor/store price.

Do NOT restore the historical order price.

Example:

Previous order:
1 kg
₹70

Current store price:
1 kg
₹75

Reorder should add:

1 kg
₹75

to the current cart.

The historical order must continue displaying its original historical price.


=================================================
7. CURRENT AVAILABILITY
=================================================

Before adding each reorder item, check the current:

- product/listing status
- variant status
- vendor/store eligibility
- availability
- purchasability
- server-side stock

Do not assume that an item purchased previously is still available.


=================================================
8. OUT-OF-STOCK REORDER ITEM
=================================================

If the previous variant is now out of stock:

Do NOT substitute another variant.

Do NOT add an unavailable item to the cart as purchasable.

Clearly tell the customer:

"Some items are currently unavailable."

Example:

Reorder result:

✓ Basmati Rice — 1 kg — Added
✓ Milk — 1 L — Added
⚠ Eggs — 12 pieces — Currently unavailable

Then:

[ View Cart ]

The customer can continue with the available items.


=================================================
9. UNAVAILABLE / DEACTIVATED PRODUCT
=================================================

If the previous product or exact variant is no longer active:

Do NOT substitute another product.

Show:

"Some items are no longer available."

Identify the unavailable item where appropriate.

Continue adding other eligible items.


=================================================
10. NO SUBSTITUTIONS
=================================================

This is a strict requirement.

Do NOT automatically replace:

Basmati Rice 1 kg

with:

Basmati Rice 5 kg

or:

Sona Masoori Rice 1 kg

or another brand/product.

Reorder must use exact product/variant identity.

If exact identity is unavailable:

mark it unavailable and inform the customer.


=================================================
11. STORE CONTEXT
=================================================

Reorder must respect the original vendor/store relationship and the current store eligibility rules.

Do not mix different vendors into the current cart.

If the previous order belongs to Store A and the customer currently has a Store B cart:

use the existing one-store cart conflict behavior.

Do NOT silently merge Store A and Store B products.


=================================================
12. EXISTING CART
=================================================

If the customer already has items in the cart and chooses Reorder:

Do NOT silently delete existing cart items.

Do NOT silently replace existing cart items.

Use the existing one-store cart rules.

If the reorder belongs to the same store:

add eligible items to the existing cart.

If it belongs to another store:

show the existing store-conflict confirmation.

Example:

"Your cart contains items from Development Store A.

This reorder is from Development Store B.

Replace your current cart?"

[ Cancel ]
[ Clear Cart & Continue ]


=================================================
13. DUPLICATE CART ITEMS
=================================================

If the exact product + exact variant is already in the cart:

Do not create a confusing duplicate line unless the existing cart architecture intentionally supports it.

Prefer increasing the cart quantity appropriately.

Example:

Current cart:
Basmati Rice
1 kg × 2

Reorder:
Basmati Rice
1 kg × 2

Result:

Basmati Rice
1 kg × 4

Do not create:

Basmati Rice 1 kg × 2
Basmati Rice 1 kg × 2

as separate lines.

Respect existing cart quantity validation.


=================================================
14. REORDER QUANTITY
=================================================

Reorder should initially use the quantity from the previous order.

Example:

Previous order:

Basmati Rice
1 kg × 3

Reorder initially adds:

Basmati Rice
1 kg × 3

The customer can then modify the quantity in Cart.

Do not create a new selling variant based on the previous order quantity.


=================================================
15. HISTORICAL ORDER DATA
=================================================

Historical orders are immutable.

Do NOT modify:

- historical product name
- historical variant name
- historical price
- historical quantity
- historical totals
- historical order snapshots

Reorder creates a NEW cart state using CURRENT catalog/store information.

Historical order remains exactly as it was.

✓ Milk — 1 L

Unavailable:

Then:

[ View Cart ]
[ Continue Shopping ]

17. ALL ITEMS UNAVAILABLE

Do NOT create an empty order.

Show:

"None of these items are currently available."

Provide:

[ Continue Shopping ]

Do not show a misleading successful reorder message.
