==================================================
PROJECT CONTEXT
==================================================

The approved architecture from Steps 1–5 is:

Platform Master Catalog
    ↓
Explicit Vendor Selection
    ↓
Existing Store-Owned Product
    ↓
Existing Store-Owned Variants
    ↓
Cart / Order / Historical Snapshots

The following architecture is already approved and MUST NOT be reconsidered:

1. Use master_categories.
2. Use master_products.
3. Existing categories remain store-owned.
4. Existing products remain store-owned listings.
5. Existing product_variants remain store-owned selling variants.
6. Add nullable master references to existing categories/products.
7. Do NOT create a replacement store_products table.
8. Do NOT move price or stock to master_products.
9. Do NOT change cart/order identity structure.
10. Do NOT rewrite historical order snapshots.
11. Vendor selections must be explicit.
12. Vendor A selecting a master product must NOT automatically make it available in Vendor B's store.
13. Customer shopping must always begin from the selected store's products.
14. Re-order must preserve original product/variant IDs and must never substitute another product.
15. Store-specific price and stock remain independent.
16. No implementation has been authorized yet.

The Step 5 audit confirmed that implementation cannot begin until the remaining behavior-critical business decisions are explicitly resolved.
