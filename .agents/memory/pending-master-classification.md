---
name: Pending Master Catalog classification
description: Why uncategorized canonical products must remain reusable rather than use a fake taxonomy category.
---

Treat an unassigned master category as pending platform classification, independently
of active/archived status. Keep active pending identities discoverable and reusable
through the vendor catalog. Classification must retain the same canonical identity.

**Why:** Existing pending records must retain their identities and remain reusable
even after master authoring becomes Admin-only.
Using a fake “Unclassified” category would mix workflow state into the real taxonomy;
hiding pending identities would make the first global search appear incomplete and
increase repeated missing-product requests.

**How to apply:** Preserve existing active pending identities in vendor browse/add
and show pending classification clearly to Admin. Only Admin may author canonical
products or assign platform categories. Approved Vendor-owned local products
may be created without creating canonical identities or categories.
Preserve archive restrictions for products assigned to
archived categories. Do not introduce a separate pending-product model or change
identity when classification is assigned.
