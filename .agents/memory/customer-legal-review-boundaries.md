---
name: Customer legal review boundaries
description: Business approval, legal review, source preservation and privacy-action limits for Customer legal pages.
---
Customer legal pages must remain clearly identified as informational drafts until
the owner confirms business identity, roles, contacts, policy decisions and any
effective date, and obtains qualified legal review.

Source-provided contacts, reporting deadlines, complaint-response targets and
future-feature clauses are not automatically approved operational commitments.
Identify unresolved proposals rather than silently adopting or deleting them.
Retain mandatory consumer protections; do not invent liability caps or venues.

**Why:** The user required faithful adaptation of the supplied Terms while
explicitly prohibiting fabricated details, unsupported promises and claims that
unfinished legal documents are publication-ready.

**How to apply:** Confirm business facts before activating a source contact or
removing a review notice. Recheck implemented capabilities when editing policy
content. Do not generate an effective date merely because a page changed.

Privacy actions may use existing supported controls only. A new account
deletion/erasure process requires defined authentication, confirmation,
order-history retention, legal obligations and related-record handling first.

**Why:** The user explicitly prohibited destructive deletion without that
process and required honest limitations where request workflows do not exist.

**How to apply:** Keep unsupported requests informational; never report that a
request was submitted or data erased without a real, safe workflow.
