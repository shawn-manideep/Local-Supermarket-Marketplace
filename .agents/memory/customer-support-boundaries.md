---
name: Customer support boundaries
description: Support content must reflect actual features and approved policies, without fictional contact channels or guarantees.
---
Customer Help & Support must describe implemented capabilities and approved
policies only. Never invent support phone numbers, email addresses, chat channels,
wallet/payment features, refund promises or round-the-clock availability.

**Why:** The user explicitly required accurate help content and honest in-app
messages when no working support destination exists.

**How to apply:** Verify the relevant Customer flow before changing an FAQ.
When adding a support destination, use the actual configured channel and do not
claim a message or ticket was sent until that action genuinely succeeds.
