---
name: Vendor compliance onboarding
description: All-Vendor approval policy, independent approval checks and existing fulfillment compatibility.
---
Required document and bank review applies to all Vendors, including existing
accounts. Operational catalog access and new orders require current Admin-approved
documents, current Admin-approved bank details, and an active approved store.

**Why:** The user explicitly approved the all-Vendor rollout, superseding the
earlier new-Vendor-only exemption.

**How to apply:** Do not grandfather existing catalog access or backfill fictional
approvals. Keep profile, compliance submissions, approval status, logout and
existing order/delivery fulfillment available under their existing authorization
rules. Preserve account identities, historical records and outstanding work.

Mandatory uploads alone do not unlock catalog access.
The Vendor must submit the current mandatory versions and receive explicit Admin
approval. A rejection needs a reason and selected mandatory types; correction and
resubmission remain gated until another approval. Submitted mandatory versions
must stay fixed while under review.

Bank changes also require a fresh review; bank and document approval are separate.

**Why:** The user requires explicit approval of the actual current submissions,
not merely upload completion or an earlier approval.

**How to apply:** Keep private, current-version ownership boundaries for reads
and review. Optional uploads remain informational and are not automatically
approved. Document review must not implicitly activate a store or change its
administrative status.

Self-registered Vendors must verify their email and complete a new store
registration before entering the Vendor portal. Their store remains pending;
document approval alone does not grant catalog access until Admin also activates
the store. Neither document nor bank approval implicitly activates a store.

**Why:** Public signup must not become an alternate route around Admin store
approval or the all-Vendor compliance gate.

**How to apply:** Treat email confirmation, account provisioning, document
review, bank review and store activation as separate checks; never infer one from
another. A submitted business contact number is not verified phone ownership.
