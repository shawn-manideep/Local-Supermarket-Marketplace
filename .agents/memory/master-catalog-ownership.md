---
name: Master Catalog ownership and cleanup
description: User-approved Admin-only authoring, no automatic sample catalog, and preservation scope for catalog cleanup.
---

Admin owns global master category/product authoring. Vendors browse, select and
add to their own stores, then independently configure selling data; they must not
create global identities. Do not automatically populate sample grocery records.

**Why:** The user wants their own platform catalog and explicitly superseded the
previous vendor missing-product creation workflow.

**How to apply:** Retain the existing canonical tables and stable IDs, including
future Excel authoring. Do not restore vendor creation as a convenience feature.

Catalog cleanup must establish demo provenance rather than classify by grocery
names. Keep vendor commercial data and historical references when retiring
masters. The user explicitly chose to preserve the newer/edited Pulses,
Atta & Flours, sabudana and Cinnamon entries; Alshanthalu was not confirmed demo.

**Why:** The controlled reset was approved only for confirmed seeded/test records,
not every current master or vendor-selected listing.

**How to apply:** Do not treat a seed timestamp alone as permission to retire an
entry subsequently edited by the owner. Preserve protected references by archival,
never hard deletion or rewriting commercial/history data.
